Before you start:
  • Use Chrome or Edge on desktop (Web Serial isn't supported in Firefox/Safari or on mobile).
  • Plug the board in with a data-capable USB-C cable — a charge-only cable won't show up as a serial port.
  • If the board doesn't appear in the port list, you may need a driver for its USB-to-serial chip (commonly CP210x or CH340) — check which one your board uses.
  • Flashing erases whatever's currently on the board, including any in-progress game — this installs fresh firmware, not an update over existing data.
